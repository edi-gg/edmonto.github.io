About

From shipping
products to
backing founders.

Originally from Romania and raised in Northern Virginia, I caught the engineering bug early — learning to code in middle school through video games. I studied Computer Science and Business at Rice University, then shipped code at Facebook and the Federal Reserve before advising Fortune 500 clients at McKinsey across energy, tech, and consumer sectors.

Now as a Senior Associate at Scale Venture Partners, I focus on AI applications — evaluating not just product-market fit, but founder-market fit. My engineering background lets me read code, assess technical architecture, and understand what it truly takes to ship. I led the build of Scale Studio, our internal data and benchmarking platform, before moving to the investment team.
